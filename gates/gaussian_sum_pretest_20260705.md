## Split and closure

- Mixture starts by recursively splitting the input Gaussian into M components along the current largest `w * lambda_max(Sigma)` axis.
- Split choice: two equal-weight children, means offset `+/- 0.70 sigma` along the top eigenvector, child variance along that axis reduced to `0.51` of the parent.
