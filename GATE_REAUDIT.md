# Re-audit of gate rejections under the corrected scoring model

Date: 2026-08-11

## Executive result

The archive does **not** contain a demonstrated, truth-clean candidate with either a materially lower variance constant than the current route's `V = 7.5e4 FLOP` or a measured `alpha > 1`. The successful QMC/LHS gates were all evaluated at one sample count; the three v1 Sobol infrastructure attempts returned zero scientific rows. Consequently, none of the QMC/lattice rejections can be reversed on an exponent argument from the existing artifacts.

There are four truth-independent replicate-variance signals below their paired baselines: odd-state Rao--Blackwell (`0.5643x` baseline variance), IID-sphere recolor (`0.9678x`), Sobol-sphere v2 (`0.99674x`), and terminal-mixture readout (`0.99868x`). The first is killed by overwhelming bias. The other three are too small to survive their unmeasured candidate overhead and have no truth-clean bias or exponent measurement. They form a provisional re-test queue, not promotion evidence.

The re-audit also found a systematic decomposition error. In the Haar fold-CV, spherical-Stein, and Sobol-sphere-v2 aggregators, the MSE of the three-replicate mean was labeled `bias_squared`, while the population variance of three estimates (divisor three) was labeled the full per-replicate `variance`. For three replicates, with `M1` the mean one-replicate MSE and `M3` the MSE of the replicate mean, the correct quantities are

```text
pair_variance = (3/2) * (M1 - M3)
bias_squared_proxy = M1 - pair_variance = (3/2) * M3 - (1/2) * M1.
```

All classifications below use raw JSONL rows when present. Numbers marked **BANK** use `analysis/truth_bank/` truth and are not used to rank MSE or bias. Replicate-pair variance is identified from differences between candidate replicates and does not use the bank truth; it is shown separately, but its conversion to `V` is optimistic where candidate compute was not metered.

## Conventions

- `M1`: mean final-layer MSE of one candidate replication.
- `sigma^2`: per-replication pair variance from independent replicate differences.
- `b^2`: squared-bias proxy. `BANK` means it is not decision-grade at the `1e-7` scale.
- `V*`: optimistic normalized variance constant, `7.5e4 * sigma_cand^2 / sigma_base^2`. It assumes equal effective compute per replicate; payload gates did not meter candidate overhead.
- `rows matched; C unknown`: arms propagated the same number of particle rows, but candidate-specific compute was not measured.
- Criterion types are exactly the requested classes: `bias-threshold`, `total-MSE`, `adjusted-score`, `ratio-gate`, or `other`.

1. `6.0066460386e-7` in `terminal_mixture_readout_gate_20260710_results.json` is the rejected **mixture candidate's** clipped per-MLP squared-bias proxy, not the default route's bias. 2. `9.819731e-7` in `haar_sphere_foldcv_gate_20260710_report.md` belongs to **`haar_cv`**, not the default route. Moreover, it is not an unbiased three-rep bias estimate at all; it is `M3`, the MSE of the candidate's three-replicate mean.

### Additional errors found in this audit

3. **Systematic three-replicate decomposition error in Haar fold-CV.** The report labels CV `bias^2=9.819731e-7` and variance `1.709173e-6`. Raw rows give correct `b^2=1.27388e-7` and pair variance `2.563760e-6`. For current, reported `1.128939e-6 / 1.548545e-6` becomes `3.54656e-7 / 2.322838e-6`. For raw Haar, reported `9.771848e-7 / 1.755585e-6` becomes `9.93926e-8 / 2.633379e-6`. Source: [raw](gates/haar_sphere_foldcv_gate_20260710_fly.jsonl), aggregation formula in [script](gates/haar_sphere_foldcv_aggregate_20260710.py).
4. **The same decomposition error in spherical Stein.** Stein's reported `bias^2=1.887585e-6`, variance `3.814727e-6` becomes raw `b^2=-1.98e-8` (consistent with zero) and pair variance `5.722090e-6`. Current becomes `3.82e-8 / 2.710549e-6`, not `9.41694e-7 / 1.807033e-6`. The gate's stated `stein_bias: FAIL` was therefore caused by the aggregation definition, although total MSE still rejects the candidate. Source: [raw](gates/spherical_stein_gate_20260710_fly.jsonl), [script](gates/spherical_stein_aggregate_20260710.py).
5. **The same decomposition error in Sobol-sphere v2.** Reported Sobol `bias^2=9.164118e-7`, variance `1.731731e-6` becomes raw `5.05465e-8 / 2.597596e-6`. IID sphere becomes `1.47673e-7 / 2.522160e-6`; current becomes `5.37199e-8 / 2.606098e-6`. The lower-variance Class-A signal survives, but the reported bias magnitudes do not. Source: [raw](gates/sobol_sphere_v2_gate_20260710_fly.jsonl), [script](gates/sobol_sphere_v2_aggregate_20260710.py).
6. **One-replicate Jacobian-LT “bias/variance” is not a decomposition.** The report assigns each arm `bias^2=MSE` and variance `0` because only one replication exists. Both quantities are unidentifiable. Source: [report](gates/sobol_jacobian_lt_v2_stagea_report_20260710.md), [raw](gates/sobol_jacobian_lt_v2_stagea_fly_20260710.jsonl).
7. **Block-count attribution in two 2026-07-07 gates.** `block_predictability` and `spline_conditional_readout` described a nominal b16 route, but their interleaved helper plus first-half slice produced only eight unique independent bases duplicated to 16 nominal blocks. The former is a duplicated-b8 proxy gate; the latter is an effective-b8 readout gate. The history already contains this correction, but the original reports remain misleading. Sources: [block raw](gates/block_predictability_gate_20260707.jsonl), [spline raw](gates/spline_conditional_readout_gate_20260707_fly.jsonl).
8. **Prefix-rank launch metadata said 27 blocks although the source and arrays used 32.** This is metadata-only and does not change the science, but the count attribution is wrong in emitted rows. Source: [report correction](gates/prefix_rank_stratified_v2_stagea_report_20260710.md).
9. **Mean-of-ratios versus ratio-of-means is easy to misread in several QMC reports.** For Sobol-sphere v2, table “mean ratio” `1.1448` is the mean of per-MLP ratios, while the global ratio of mean MSEs is `2.659818/2.648143=1.00441`. Haar and Sobol-Gaussian reports make the same distinction in some places but not consistently in prose. Gate decisions used separate global/median fields; the headline mean ratio should not be treated as a global variance ratio.

The corrected three-replicate quantities are still **BANK** for bias/MSE purposes. Their value here is to fix arm and quantity attribution and to recover truth-independent replicate variance, not to rank bank-derived bias.

## Final disposition

No archived candidate demonstrates a promotion-grade lower `V`, and no QMC/lattice candidate has the multi-count data needed to show `alpha > 1`. The only nontrivial lower-variance signal is the IID-sphere recolor control at an optimistic `V*=7.258e4`, but its full-budget bias allowance is below `9e-9`, its bias artifact is contaminated, and its compute is unmetered. Sobol-sphere and terminal mixture are still closer to noise at `V*=7.476e4` and `7.490e4`.

Thus the clean operational answer is negative: the archive does not presently justify reviving a rejected estimator family. At most it justifies a new, truth-clean, metered, multi-count measurement of IID-sphere/Sobol-sphere recolor before closing that last variance-only ambiguity. Everything else redirects effort to unsearched families.
