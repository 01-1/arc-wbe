# Research notes

Closeouts and scouting notes written while a lane was being explored. A
**closeout** records why a direction was abandoned — usually an analytic
obstruction or a measured ceiling — and is written to stop the same idea being
picked up again. Several close a family on theory alone, with no gate attached.

These are working notes, not polished write-ups. Figures in them predate the
[gate re-audit](../GATE_REAUDIT.md), which found nine label-level errors
elsewhere in this archive; re-derive anything load-bearing from raw rows.
